Getting Started

Create your account and log in

Sign Up

Visit the Sign Up page and fill in the required details:

FieldDescription
Full NameYour name as it appears on school records
Email AddressA valid email you have access to
Matric NumberYour university matriculation number
FacultyE.g. Engineering, Sciences, Arts
DepartmentE.g. Computer Science, Accounting
PasswordA strong, secure password
Quick tip

Sign up instantly with your Google account — just click "Continue with Google" and no password is required.

Log In

Visit the Login page, enter your email and password, then click "Sign in to your account".

  • Tick "Remember me" to stay logged in across sessions.
  • Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password?" — a reset link will be sent to your email.
  • You can also log in with Google via the Google button.

Courses

Add, edit and manage all your courses

The Courses section gives you a full list of every course you've registered. All changes sync to the cloud instantly.

How to Add a Course

  1. Click "Courses" in the sidebar (or bottom nav on mobile).

  2. Click the "+ Add Course" button.

  3. Fill in the Course Title, Course Code, Credit Units, and your Grade.

  4. Select which Semester the course belongs to.

  5. Click Save.

Academic Planner

Your personal task manager for school activities

The Academic Planner lets you track assignments, exam dates, and project deadlines in one place.

How to use it

  • Click "+ Add Task" to create a new task.
  • Enter a task name, due date, and priority level (Low, Medium, or High).
  • Check the checkbox next to a task to mark it as completed.
  • Click the trash icon to delete tasks you no longer need.

Course Resources

Save links and materials for each course

The Course Resources section lets you bookmark and organise study materials — textbook PDFs, YouTube videos, past questions, and more.

How to add a resource

  1. Click "+ Add Resource".

  2. Enter a resource name (e.g. "MTH 101 Textbook PDF").

  3. Paste the URL — link to the document, video, or website.

  4. Assign it to a course.

  5. Click Save. You can click any resource to open it directly.

Semester Archives

Review your historical semester performance

The Semester Archives is a read-only historical view of all your past semesters.

  • Each semester card shows its GPA and number of courses.
  • A bar chart visually compares your GPA across all semesters.
  • Expand each semester card to see all individual courses and their grades.
Note

Archives is a read-only view. To edit courses or grades, go to the Courses section.

Using on Mobile

SpiceCGPA Pro is fully optimised for phones

  • Use the bottom navigation bar to switch between Dashboard, Courses, Analytics, and Settings.
  • Tap the ☰ menu icon (top left) to open the full navigation drawer.
  • Tap the green ➕ floating button to quickly add a new course from anywhere.
  • Enter your raw score (e.g. 75) and the app automatically assigns the correct letter grade.
  • Tap your profile icon (top right) to quickly access your profile settings.
